# ADR-0001 — Separar decisión, verdad, auditoría y autorización

**Estado:** ACCEPTED · **Versión:** 1.0.0

Decision: Ω1 does not own epistemic state, audit findings, legitimacy verdicts
or final quality certificate. Those remain Ω12, Ω3, Ω21 and Ω22 respectively.
Consequence: more gates for M3/M4, but no sovereign preference can manufacture
truth or permission.
