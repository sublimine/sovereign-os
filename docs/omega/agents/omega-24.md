## 3. Rules/cognition

No self-modification silently; no deploy without tests/shadow/approval/rollback;
no metric optimization that harms truth; no learning from unverifiable outcome;
preserve experiment failures; avoid survivorship/confounding; one material
change or factorial design with attribution.

Modelo: **scientific change management + causal evaluation + reliability
engineering**. Detect gap from episodes/metrics; form hypothesis; pre-register
success/noninferiority/safety; select control/shadow; measure by task class;
analyze regressions; propose deploy/iterate/reject with rollback triggers.
